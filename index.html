About

Since 2024, I have been a tenure-track assistant professor at the Guangxi Center for Mathematics Research, Guangxi University.

I received my Ph.D. from the School of Mathematical Sciences at the University of Chinese Academy of Sciences, under the supervision of Professor Fusheng Deng . Prior to joining Guangxi University, I was a postdoctoral fellow at the Institute of Mathematics, Chinese Academy of Sciences , where I worked under the supervision of Professor Xiangyu Zhou .
