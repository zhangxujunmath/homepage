Xujun Zhang / 张旭俊

Assistant Professor

Guangxi Center for Mathematics Research, School of Mathematics, Guangxi University

I am a tenure-track assistant professor at Guangxi Center for Mathematics Research, Guangxi University since 2024.

I received my Ph.D. at School of Mathematical Sciences at University of Chinese Academy of Sciences under the supervision of Professor Fusheng Deng. Previously, I was a postdoc fellow at Institute of Mathematics at Chinese Academy of Sciences supervised by Professor Xiangyu Zhou.
