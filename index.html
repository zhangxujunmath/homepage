Xujun Zhang 张旭俊

Tenure-Track Assistant Professor

About

I am a tenure-track assistant professor at the Guangxi Center for Mathematics Research, Guangxi University (since 2024).

I received my Ph.D. from the School of Mathematical Sciences at the University of Chinese Academy of Sciences (UCAS), under the supervision of Professor Fusheng Deng. Previously, I was a postdoctoral researcher at the Institute of Mathematics, Chinese Academy of Sciences, working with Professor Xiangyu Zhou.
